Requesting Deletion

To request deletion of personal information controlled by Lost Banner Studio:

  • Email lostbannerstudio@gmail.com.
  • Use the subject: “Data Deletion Request – Banner of Ash.”
  • Describe the information you want deleted, such as previous support emails, attachments, or other information you sent directly to Lost Banner Studio.

We may request reasonable information to identify the relevant communication and verify that the request came from the correct person. Do not send passwords, payment-card information, or your Google account password.

Data That Can Be Deleted

Lost Banner Studio can delete information it directly controls, including:

  • Customer-support emails;
  • Attachments and information voluntarily provided through support communications; and
  • Records directly associated with the privacy request, except where limited retention is legally required.

Local Game Data

Game progress, settings, daily rewards, and other gameplay information are stored locally on your device and are not accessible through a Lost Banner Studio server.

You can delete local game data by:

  • Using an available reset option inside the Game;
  • Opening your Android device settings, selecting Apps, selecting Banner of Ash, and clearing the Game’s storage; or
  • Uninstalling the Game.

Clearing storage or uninstalling the Game may permanently remove game progress.

Third-Party Data

Google AdMob, Google Play, and Unity may process information independently under their own privacy policies. Lost Banner Studio cannot directly delete information controlled independently by these providers.

You can reset or delete your Android Advertising ID through your device’s privacy or advertising settings. Menu names may vary depending on the Android version and device manufacturer.

Purchase and payment records maintained by Google Play are governed by Google’s policies and may be retained for transaction, fraud-prevention, tax, legal, or accounting purposes.

Retention Period

Lost Banner Studio will normally process valid deletion requests within 30 days. Minimal records relating to a completed request may be retained for up to 90 days for security and record-keeping purposes. Information may be retained longer where required by law, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or enforcement of legal rights.
