1. INTRODUCTION

This Privacy Policy explains how Lost Banner Studio ("Lost Banner Studio," "we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with the Banner of Ash mobile game (the "Game").

Banner of Ash is a mobile strategy game that includes advertisements and optional in-app purchases. The Game does not require players to create an account or sign in. Game progress, settings, and daily-reward information are stored locally on the player's device.

By downloading or using the Game, you acknowledge the information practices described in this Privacy Policy. Where consent is required by applicable law, we or our service providers will request it separately.

3. INFORMATION WE PROCESS

3.1 Information You Provide

The Game does not require you to provide a name, email address, telephone number, profile, or other account information.

If you contact Lost Banner Studio for customer support or to make a privacy request, we receive the information contained in your communication, such as your email address and any information you choose to provide. We use this information to respond to you, investigate the matter, protect the Game, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

3.2 Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Banner of Ash stores gameplay information locally on your device. This may include:

  • Saved game progress;
  • Resources, buildings, units, research, and other gameplay state;
  • Game settings and preferences;
  • Daily rewards, login streaks, and related reward information; and
  • Purchase entitlements required to provide purchased game content.

Lost Banner Studio does not receive this local gameplay information through a game server or cloud-save service.

You can remove locally stored information by using an available in-game reset option, clearing the Game's storage through your device settings, or uninstalling the Game. Uninstalling or clearing storage may permanently remove progress that cannot be recovered, except for eligible purchases that can be restored through Google Play.

3.3 Information Collected Through Google AdMob

Banner of Ash uses Google AdMob to display and measure advertisements. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share certain information for advertising, analytics, security, and fraud-prevention purposes, including:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) address, which may be used to estimate approximate location;
  • Device and account identifiers, including the Android Advertising ID and App Set ID where available;
  • Advertising and product interactions, such as app launches, taps, ad impressions, and video views; and
  • Diagnostic and performance information, such as launch time, errors reported by the SDK, hang rate, and energy usage.

Banner of Ash does not collect precise GPS location through the Game. Google states that information collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted during transmission.

3.4 In-App Purchase Information

Banner of Ash offers optional in-app purchases using Unity In-App Purchasing and Google Play's billing system. Google Play processes payments. Lost Banner Studio does not receive your complete credit-card number, bank-account number, or Google account password.

The Game and Google Play may process purchase-related information, such as the purchased product, transaction status, receipt or purchase token, and entitlement state. This information is used to complete, verify, restore, support, and prevent fraud involving purchases.

4. HOW INFORMATION IS USED

Information described in this Privacy Policy may be used to:

  • Operate, maintain, secure, and troubleshoot the Game;
  • Display, personalize where permitted, and measure advertisements;
  • Detect and prevent fraud, invalid advertising traffic, abuse, and security incidents;
  • Process, verify, and restore in-app purchases;
  • Respond to customer support and privacy requests;
  • Understand technical performance and resolve errors; and
  • Comply with applicable laws, legal processes, and enforceable government requests.

5. LEGAL BASES FOR PROCESSING

Where the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the United Kingdom GDPR, or similar laws apply, processing may be based on:

  • Consent, including for personalized advertising and other processing that requires consent;
  • Performance of a contract or steps requested by you, including providing and restoring purchases;
  • Legitimate interests, including operating and securing the Game, preventing fraud, responding to support requests, and serving legally permitted forms of advertising; and
  • Compliance with legal obligations.

Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw your consent at any time through the available privacy controls. Withdrawal does not affect processing that was lawful before consent was withdrawn.

6. ADVERTISING CONSENT AND PRIVACY CHOICES

Where required, Banner of Ash uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to display advertising disclosures and collect privacy choices before requesting ads.

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may be able to:

  • Consent to the listed advertising purposes and providers;
  • Decline consent;
  • Manage individual purposes and advertising providers; and
  • Review or change your selection later through the Privacy Options control in the Game's settings when that control is required.

If you do not consent, Google may provide limited or less-personalized advertising where permitted.

You can also reset or delete your Android Advertising ID through your device's privacy settings. The exact menu location may differ depending on your Android version and device manufacturer.

7. WHEN INFORMATION IS SHARED

Information may be disclosed in the following circumstances:

  • To Google and participating advertising technology providers for advertising, measurement, security, and fraud-prevention purposes;
  • To Google Play and Unity technologies used to deliver and support in-app purchases;
  • To service providers that process information to perform services for Lost Banner Studio;
  • When reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, legal process, or enforceable government requests; and
  • When necessary to protect the rights, safety, property, and security of players, Lost Banner Studio, service providers, or other persons.

Lost Banner Studio does not sell personal information for monetary payment. Certain disclosures associated with personalized or cross-context behavioral advertising may be treated as a "sale" or "sharing" under some United States privacy laws. Where applicable, you may manage these activities using the Game's Privacy Options control or your Android advertising settings.

9. DATA RETENTION AND DELETION

Local gameplay information remains on your device until it is reset, the Game's storage is cleared, or the Game is uninstalled. Lost Banner Studio cannot remotely access or delete information that exists only in your local save.

Customer support communications and privacy requests are retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain appropriate records, resolve disputes, protect the Game, and meet legal obligations.

Google, Unity, and other third-party providers retain information according to their own privacy policies and legal obligations. You may need to contact the relevant provider or use its privacy tools regarding information it controls independently.

10. INTERNATIONAL DATA TRANSFERS

Google, Unity, and other service providers may process information in countries other than the country in which you live. Where required by applicable law, appropriate safeguards are used for international transfers as described in the relevant provider's privacy documentation.

11. DATA SECURITY

Information transmitted by the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Unity In-App Purchasing is encrypted in transit using TLS or comparable security protections. Lost Banner Studio also uses reasonable administrative and technical measures appropriate to the information it handles. However, no electronic storage or transmission method is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

12. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Banner of Ash is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under the age of 13. Lost Banner Studio does not knowingly request personal information directly from children under 13.

If you believe that a child under 13 has provided personal information directly to Lost Banner Studio, please contact us so that we can review and delete the information where appropriate.

Players below the age at which they can independently consent to data processing in their country should use the Game with the involvement of a parent or legal guardian. Advertising settings may be restricted where a player is identified as being below the applicable age of consent.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights relating to personal information processed directly by Lost Banner Studio. These rights may include the right to:

  • Request access to personal information;
  • Request correction or deletion;
  • Restrict or object to certain processing;
  • Receive a portable copy of eligible information;
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent; and
  • Lodge a complaint with an applicable data-protection authority.

Eligible residents of California and other United States jurisdictions may also have rights to know, correct, or delete covered personal information; opt out of certain sales or sharing; limit certain uses of sensitive personal information; and receive equal service when exercising privacy rights. These rights are subject to applicable legal definitions, exceptions, and eligibility requirements.

To submit a request concerning information controlled directly by Lost Banner Studio, contact us using the information in Section 2 or Section 15. We may request information reasonably necessary to verify and process your request. Requests involving information controlled independently by Google or another third party may need to be submitted directly to that provider.
